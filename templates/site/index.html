A shutter that runs slow when cold

July 2, 2026

Old leaf shutters thicken with age. A careful cleaning of the blades with naphtha brought the 1/250 back from what was closer to 1/60.

Fixer exhaustion, measured

The clip test is cheap: a strip of leader in fresh fixer clears in thirty seconds. Once it takes twice as long, the batch goes to recycling.
